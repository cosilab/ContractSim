==================================================
  AGREED CONTRACT
==================================================
Parties: Customer and Caterer.
Term: Weeks 1 through 11.
Unit prices: Margherita Pizza $6; Pesto Pasta $5; Tomato Soup $3.
Delivery obligations:
Week 2: 4 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 4: 4 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 6: 4 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 8: 4 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 10: 4 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
A shortfall is the amount by which a production week's delivery falls below its delivery obligation.
Payments: Week 1 $81; Week 3 $57; Week 5 $22; Week 7 $25; Week 9 $15; Week 11 $0.
Substitution: After delivering the non-substitutable minimum for each dish (1 Margherita Pizza, 1 Pesto Pasta, 1 Tomato Soup), the Caterer may substitute quantities delivered above those minimums at 1 Margherita Pizza = 2 Pesto Pastas = 4 Tomato Soups, but only if the substitution fully satisfies that production week's delivery obligation. Partial fulfilment via substitution receives no credit; any resulting shortfall is calculated from actual physical deliveries and handled under the contract's other applicable clauses.
Termination: Except as modified by the clauses above, a violation by a party entitles the counterparty to cease all further performance immediately and permanently.
