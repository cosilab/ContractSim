==================================================
  AGREED CONTRACT
==================================================
Parties: Customer and Caterer.
Term: Weeks 1 through 11.
Unit prices: Margherita Pizza $11; Pesto Pasta $5; Tomato Soup $3.
Delivery obligations:
Week 2: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 3 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 4: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 3 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 6: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 3 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 8: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 3 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 10: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 3 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
A shortfall is the amount by which a production week's delivery falls below its delivery obligation.
Payments: Week 1 $63; Week 3 $68; Week 5 $19; Week 7 $30; Week 9 $0; Week 11 $20.
Substitution: After delivering the non-substitutable minimum for each dish (1 Margherita Pizza, 1 Pesto Pasta, 1 Tomato Soup), the Caterer may substitute quantities delivered above those minimums at 1 Margherita Pizza = 2 Pesto Pastas = 4 Tomato Soups, but only if the substitution fully satisfies that production week's delivery obligation. Partial fulfilment via substitution receives no credit; any resulting shortfall is calculated from actual physical deliveries and handled under the contract's other applicable clauses.
Rollover: A shortfall by the Caterer of no more than 2 units in each dish is added, dish by dish, to the next production week's delivery obligations to be cured. Carried shortfalls cannot be substituted. It is a violation if the shortfall in any dish exceeds 2 units, if the Caterer has a shortfall in two consecutive production weeks, or if a shortfall occurs in the final production week.
Payment deduction: The Customer may reduce the next scheduled payment by the unit-price value of that production round's shortfall, to no less than $0; any deduction in excess of that payment is forfeited and does not carry to later payments; the deduction is final and creates no later repayment obligation. Absent a rollover clause, the Caterer need not make up the undelivered units. Two consecutive production weeks with a shortfall by the Caterer is a violation.
Termination: Except as modified by the clauses above, a violation by a party entitles the counterparty to cease all further performance immediately and permanently.
