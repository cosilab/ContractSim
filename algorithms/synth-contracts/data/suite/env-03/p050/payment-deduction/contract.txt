==================================================
  AGREED CONTRACT
==================================================
Parties: Customer and Caterer.
Term: Weeks 1 through 11.
Unit prices: Margherita Pizza $6; Pesto Pasta $5; Tomato Soup $2.
Delivery obligations:
Week 2: 1 Margherita Pizza, 6 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 4: 1 Margherita Pizza, 6 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 6: 1 Margherita Pizza, 6 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 8: 1 Margherita Pizza, 6 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
Week 10: 1 Margherita Pizza, 6 Pesto Pastas, 2 Tomato Soups.
A shortfall is the amount by which a production week's delivery falls below its delivery obligation.
Payments: Week 1 $37; Week 3 $35; Week 5 $24; Week 7 $28; Week 9 $27; Week 11 $49.
Payment deduction: The Customer may reduce the next scheduled payment by the unit-price value of that production round's shortfall, to no less than $0; any deduction in excess of that payment is forfeited and does not carry to later payments; the deduction is final and creates no later repayment obligation. Absent a rollover clause, the Caterer need not make up the undelivered units. Two consecutive production weeks with a shortfall by the Caterer is a violation.
Termination: Except as modified by the clauses above, a violation by a party entitles the counterparty to cease all further performance immediately and permanently.
