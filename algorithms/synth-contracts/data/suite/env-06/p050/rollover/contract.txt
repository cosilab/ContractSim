==================================================
  AGREED CONTRACT
==================================================
Parties: Customer and Caterer.
Term: Weeks 1 through 11.
Unit prices: Margherita Pizza $11; Pesto Pasta $5; Tomato Soup $3.
Delivery obligations:
Week 2: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 4: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 6: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 8: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
Week 10: 2 Margherita Pizzas, 2 Pesto Pastas, 1 Tomato Soup.
A shortfall is the amount by which a production week's delivery falls below its delivery obligation.
Payments: Week 1 $31; Week 3 $0; Week 5 $55; Week 7 $35; Week 9 $4; Week 11 $50.
Rollover: A shortfall by the Caterer of no more than 2 units in each dish is added, dish by dish, to the next production week's delivery obligations to be cured. Carried shortfalls cannot be substituted. It is a violation if the shortfall in any dish exceeds 2 units, if the Caterer has a shortfall in two consecutive production weeks, or if a shortfall occurs in the final production week.
Termination: Except as modified by the clauses above, a violation by a party entitles the counterparty to cease all further performance immediately and permanently.
