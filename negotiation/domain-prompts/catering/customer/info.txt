SCHEDULE:

The game lasts 11 weeks. Odd weeks (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11) are PAYMENT weeks — you make a payment (6 total). Even weeks (2, 4, 6, 8, 10) are PRODUCTION weeks — the caterer buys ingredients and delivers dishes (5 total).

CONTRACT EXECUTION ACTIONS:

During contract execution, the only action at your disposal is making payments. Payments must be non-negative whole-dollar amounts, including $0.

The only actions at the caterer's disposal are: (i) buying ingredients, and (ii) producing dishes.

There is no refund mechanism in this system.

There is no communication between you and the caterer after a contract has been agreed upon, hence no renegotiation.

OBJECTIVES:

Your priority is maximizing your expected private utility, where the utility is your remaining budget plus the cumulative private value of dishes.
